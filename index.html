About Us

A small shop with a big heart

Happy Tails Grooming is a family-run shop in Denver. We started with one table, one tub and a love for dogs, and we still believe in doing things the slow, gentle way.

Every dog gets one-on-one attention from start to finish. No cages, no rushing, and no harsh products, just patient groomers, natural shampoos and plenty of belly rubs.

  • Certified, fear-free trained groomers
  • Natural, hypoallergenic products
  • Photo update when your pup is ready
